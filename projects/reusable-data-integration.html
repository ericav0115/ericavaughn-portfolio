Reusable Enterprise Data Integration

A reusable, bidirectional pattern that connects business application data with a cloud lakehouse, so teams can analyze, enrich, and write back records without rebuilding every interface.

01 · The challenge

Every new dataset meant another one-off pipeline.

One-off integrations multiply mappings, authentication logic, and maintenance work. Bidirectional updates add consistency and ownership risks on top of that.

02 · What I did

What I owned and built.

  • Developed a reusable Dataverse-to-Databricks integration approach for reading and syncing application records, with repeatable incremental or snapshot patterns.
  • Handled API connectivity, data mapping, transformation, and write-back considerations across systems.
  • Built in operational safeguards for incremental loads, reconciliation, credential handling, and failure recovery.
  • Structured the pattern so it can be reused across new entities, applications, and environments.

04 · Key decisions

The judgment behind the build.

Why a pattern instead of a pipeline?

The tenth integration should cost a fraction of the first. Making connection, auth, and mapping reusable is what gets you there.

Why support both incremental and snapshot?

Some entities change constantly and some are small reference sets. Offering both lets each one use the right tool.

Why be careful with write-back?

Two-way sync blurs who owns a record. Explicit ownership rules and reconciliation keep both systems trustworthy.
