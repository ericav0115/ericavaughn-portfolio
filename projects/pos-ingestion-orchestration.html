Retail POS Ingestion Orchestration

A retail feed sends several email notifications a day about the same prior-day point-of-sale extract. This workflow decides whether downstream processing should actually start.

RoleData engineer & workflow developer
ScaleMultiple notifications per day, one load
StatusDeveloped and tested; production hardening under review.
StackAzure Logic Apps · Azure Blob Storage · Azure Data Factory · JSON · CSV parsing

In this case study

The challenge What I did Architecture Key decisions Impact What’s next

01 · The challenge

Five emails. One file. Don’t load it five times.

Triggering the pipeline for every notification risked repeated loads, duplicate processing, and no clear answer to “is today’s data in?”

02 · What I did

What I owned and built.

  • Parsed incoming report messages to pull out the business activity date and retailer identifier.
  • Checked a date-specific blob control marker before triggering downstream ingestion.
  • Added create-if-absent control-file behavior so duplicate or repeated notifications are handled safely.
  • Integrated the flow with an Azure Data Factory pipeline and investigated polling behavior during testing.

03 · Architecture

How it fits together.

04 · Key decisions

The judgment behind the build.

Why a control marker?

It turns “did we already load this?” into a cheap, explicit check, and it doubles as a record of what was processed.

Why key on the business activity date?

Notifications arrive whenever they arrive. The activity date is what the data is actually about.

Why create-if-absent?

Two notifications landing at once shouldn’t both win. An atomic create gives one clear owner of the run.
