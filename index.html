About

I work in the space between a business problem and a production system.

My career has moved from advanced analytics and forecasting into data engineering, cloud architecture, enterprise integration, internal applications, platform reliability, and technical leadership.

I am often most useful when the problem is ambiguous: the data lives in multiple systems, the business language does not match the technical model, stakeholders need confidence, and someone has to make the path clear.

That is where I do my best work — understanding the problem, connecting the people and systems involved, making the architecture understandable, and helping a solution get all the way into production.
